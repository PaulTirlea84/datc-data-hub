# AI Data Analytics

## AI Competency

AI-enabled Analytics practitioners enhance reporting, insight generation, and decision support by leveraging AI and LLM tools. They combine traditional BI methods with AI-assisted data transformation, KPI creation, anomaly detection, and automated storytelling. The role accelerates analytical tasks, improves accuracy, and enables smarter decision-making across the organisation.

**Primary AI tools:** Power BI with Copilot · Fabric Copilot · Tableau Cloud with AI · AWS QuickSight with Amazon Q

**Key responsibilities:**

- Use AI tools to accelerate report development, KPI calculation, and insight generation
- Automate repetitive analytical tasks: data summarisation, layout suggestions, narrative generation
- Apply AI-assisted techniques: anomaly detection, forecasting, clustering, automated insights
- Validate all AI-generated outputs before applying them to any deliverable
- Build reusable prompt patterns for repeatable analytical processes
- Collaborate with Data Engineering on AI-powered data pipelines that feed reporting layers
- Guide peers on effective AI use in reporting and BI workflows

??? note "P1 — AI Assisted"

    Uses AI tools (Copilot, LLMs) for basic tasks: summarising data, exploring datasets, generating simple transformation code (DAX, SQL, M), getting layout suggestions, and drafting KPI definitions. Follows pre-built prompts and templates. Validates outputs with senior guidance before applying.
    Understands the foundational BI layer: data model structure, measure logic, report layout principles, and the relationship between semantic models and visuals. Begins learning how AI tools fit into the reporting workflow — where they accelerate, and where human validation is non-negotiable.
    Uses Copilot suggestions as a learning accelerant, not a replacement for understanding the underlying logic. Can explain what generated DAX or SQL does and why — cannot yet independently assess whether it is correct for the business requirement.
    **Typical tasks at P1:** generating basic DAX measures with Copilot, using Q&A features to explore data, drafting KPI definitions with AI assistance, following existing prompt templates for insight generation.

??? note "P2 — AI Accelerated"

    Uses LLMs to speed up reporting work: KPI calculations, dashboard logic, layout improvements, insight generation. Validates AI-generated findings independently. Begins adapting prompts for accuracy and consistency. Automates repetitive analytical tasks. AI meaningfully reduces time on standard report development.
    Uses Power BI Copilot and Fabric Copilot regularly in the development workflow — generating measures, summaries, and visual suggestions. Understands when to accept, adapt, or discard AI-generated output. Can modify AI-generated DAX or M code to meet specific requirements without full rewrites.
    **Typical tasks at P2:** using Copilot to generate and refine DAX measures independently, automating insight generation from semantic models, adapting prompts to improve output quality, accelerating dashboard layout with AI suggestions.

??? note "P3 — Agentic AI"

    Designs and uses agentic AI workflows to automate multi-step analytical tasks (transform → calculate → summarise → narrate). Creates reusable prompt patterns for repeatable analytical processes. Applies AI-assisted techniques: anomaly detection, forecasting, clustering, automated insights. Guides and supports peers on effective AI use in reporting.
    Leverages advanced Copilot features: Smart Narratives, DAX query generation, automated report summaries. Uses Tableau AI features (Explain Data, Forecasting) and QuickSight with Amazon Q where applicable. Coaches peers through workshops or pair sessions on agentic BI patterns.
    **Typical tasks at P3:** building multi-step AI workflows for end-to-end report automation, designing prompt libraries for repeatable processes, implementing anomaly detection and forecasting, leading coaching sessions on AI-enabled analytics.

??? note "P4 — Integrated AI"

    Embeds AI logic directly into dashboards, KPI frameworks, and semantic data models. Builds scalable frameworks and reusable prompt libraries for the analytics team. Leads AI adoption practices across projects. Collaborates with Data Engineering on AI-powered data pipelines that feed reporting and insight layers.
    Integrates AI tooling as a **standard part of team delivery** — embedded in report templates, semantic model standards, and review processes. Creates governance documentation for AI-generated analytics in regulated environments. Mentors specialist-level analysts on AI-augmented delivery patterns.
    **Typical tasks at P4:** leading AI-embedded semantic model design, defining team-wide AI tooling standards for analytics, reviewing AI-assisted deliverables from seniors, presenting analytics AI strategy to clients, coaching multiple analysts simultaneously.

??? note "P5 — Governed AI"

    Defines enterprise-wide AI analytics practices and governance. Advises leadership on AI opportunities in decision-making and reporting. Shapes standards and best practices for responsible AI use in analytics. Guides teams organisation-wide on integrating AI-driven insights into business workflows.
    Acts as the **recognised authority** on AI-enabled analytics at the practice level — final escalation point for complex AI design decisions, tool selection, and governance questions. Drives transformation: new capabilities, new tools, new talent pipelines for the evolving AI analytics stack.
    **Typical tasks at P5:** advising clients on AI analytics strategy, defining practice-wide governance policies for AI-generated insights, representing the team in global Data & AI communities, shaping hiring and capability-building strategy for the domain.

## AI Role Details

Builds and governs the reporting and insight layer — dashboards, semantic models, KPI frameworks, and AI-driven analytics.

??? abstract "AI Data Analytics — Career Progression"
    - **Associate** — foundational Power BI and reporting work under guidance. Uses Copilot for code generation with senior validation. Learns data model concepts and basic report quality patterns · **P1**
    - **Analyst** — independent report delivery, DAX and M ownership, AI-assisted development. Uses Power BI Copilot and Fabric Copilot regularly. Validates AI output independently · **P2**
    - **Senior Analyst** — owns report lifecycle end-to-end, introduces AI tooling into team workflows. Applies advanced Copilot features and AI-assisted insight generation · **P3**
    - **Specialist** — deep expert in BI architecture and analytics. Sets reporting standards. Designs agentic analytical workflows. Builds reusable prompt frameworks. Coaches peers · **P3**
    - **Associate Manager** — leads the analytics stream, owns delivery and platform decisions. Embeds AI tooling into team standards and review processes. Owns architecture for AI analytics initiatives · **P4**
    - **Manager** — sets analytical direction, drives AI adoption strategy, owns domain standards. Advises leadership on AI opportunities. Represents team in external forums · **P4**
    - **Architect** — defines enterprise-wide AI analytics strategy and standards. Shapes vision for data-to-insight governance and responsible AI. Acts as recognised authority · **P5**

### P5 — Governed AI
*✦ Architect*

P5 is not course-based. Completing all P4 courses is an implicit prerequisite — but not sufficient on its own.

??? note "What P5 looks like"
    Defines enterprise-wide AI analytics practices and governance. Advises leadership on AI opportunities in decision-making and reporting. Shapes standards and best practices for responsible AI use in analytics. Acts as the recognised authority on AI-enabled analytics at the practice level — final escalation point for complex AI design decisions, tool selection, and governance questions. Drives transformation: new capabilities, new tools, new talent pipelines for the evolving AI analytics stack.

**Evidence — what to demonstrate:**

**Built or designed**

- Defined team-wide standards for AI-assisted analytics (prompt libraries, Copilot governance rules, AI review checklists) — adopted across 3+ projects
- Designed and published a reusable AI analytics framework (semantic model + Copilot integration patterns) used by the team
- Created governance documentation for AI-generated analytics in a regulated client environment (KPI validation process, bias review checklist)

**Led or governed**

- Owned the AI analytics review process across 3+ concurrent projects
- Defined the team's policy for AI-generated insights and KPI validation — adopted at practice or client level
- Coached multiple analysts through P3 → P4 transition simultaneously (2+ engineers, documented outcomes)
- Led an AI analytics quality or compliance audit on a client engagement

**External or community**

- Presented AI analytics strategy to client C-suite, Accenture leadership, or a global Data & AI community
- Contributed a methodology paper, CoP presentation, or internal knowledge article on AI-driven analytics patterns
- Shaped hiring or capability-building strategy for the AI analytics domain
